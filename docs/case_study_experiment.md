\# Case Study: Evaluating a New Video Ranking Model with SplitLab



\## Problem

A short-video feed is deciding whether to replace its current ranking model with a new one. Shipping a worse model quietly erodes watch time and retention across the whole user base; holding back a genuinely better model costs growth. The platform needs a reliable way to tell the two apart before a full rollout.



\## Hypothesis

The new ranking model increases watch time per user by at least 2% without harming retention, creator diversity, or latency.
